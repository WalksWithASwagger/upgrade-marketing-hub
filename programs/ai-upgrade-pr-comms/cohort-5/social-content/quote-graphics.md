## Design Guidelines

### Color Palettes

**Ethics Forward** (Primary for this cohort)
- Background: Deep Navy (#1a1a2e)
- Accent: Warning Orange (#ff6b35)
- Text: Clean White (#ffffff)
- Use for: Ethics-focused quotes, landmine warnings

**Thought Leadership**
- Background: Slate Grey (#2d3436)
- Accent: Electric Blue (#0984e3)
- Text: Off-White (#f5f5f5)
- Use for: Framework quotes, insights

**Authentic Voice**
- Background: Warm Charcoal (#2c2c2c)
- Accent: Gold (#ffd93d)
- Text: Cream (#faf3e0)
- Use for: Personal/raw quotes from instructors

**PR Professional**
- Background: Deep Teal (#004d40)
- Accent: Silver (#b0bec5)
- Text: White (#ffffff)
- Use for: Industry-specific insights

### Layout Options

1. **Full Bleed**: Quote centered, minimal design
2. **Card Style**: Quote in card with rounded corners, speaker info below
3. **Split Screen**: Quote on dark side, attribution on light side

### Typography

- **Quote Text**: Inter Bold or Montserrat SemiBold
- **Attribution**: Outfit Light or Inter Regular
- **Size**: 32-48pt for quotes, 14-18pt for attribution

### Platform Specifications

| Platform | Size | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Instagram | 1080x1080 | Square, high contrast |
| LinkedIn | 1200x627 | Landscape, professional |
| Twitter/X | 1600x900 | Landscape, clean |
| Stories | 1080x1920 | Vertical, bold text |
