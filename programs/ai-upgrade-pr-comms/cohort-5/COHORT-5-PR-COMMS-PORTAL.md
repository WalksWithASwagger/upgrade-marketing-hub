## 🎯 Frameworks Introduced

### The Four Ethics Landmines
1. **Hallucinations** - AI states wrong things with supreme confidence
2. **Copyright** - Data ingestion, licensing, reputational risk
3. **Bias & Sycophancy** - Embedded societal biases + AI's need to please
4. **Privacy** - Data exists on platforms; hacks happen; legal compulsion possible

### The Eight-Part AI Policy Framework
1. Purpose and scope definition
2. Use cases (permitted vs. prohibited)
3. Disclosure requirements (context-dependent)
4. Human accountability and verification
5. Societal impact (environmental)
6. Organizational impact (staffing concerns)
7. Governance and enforcement
8. Update procedures

### Model Personality Framework
- **GPT-4.5**: Leading frontier model
- **Gemini**: Recently advanced, often free
- **Claude**: Projects feature for assistants
- **Perplexity**: Dominates live web search
- **Copilot**: Microsoft suite integration
