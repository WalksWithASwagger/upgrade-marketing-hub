## 🎯 Cohort Overview

Cohort 7 represents a watershed moment in the AI Upgrade journey: the normalization of **app generation as a creative capability**. Running in late 2025, this cohort bridged the gap between "using tools" and "building software," with participants shipping functional applications, workflows, and systems by the final session.

### Key Themes
- 🎨 **Vibe Coding**: Building software through natural language and aesthetic curation
- 🌐 **Indigenomics Integration**: A dedicated cluster focused on economic reconciliation and data sovereignty
- 🤖 **The Agentic Spectrum**: Framework distinguishing Assistants → Automations → Agents
- 🚀 **Production Reality**: Moving from concepts to deployed tools
