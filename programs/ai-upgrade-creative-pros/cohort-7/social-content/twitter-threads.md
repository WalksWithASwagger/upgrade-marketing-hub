## Thread 5: The Agentic Spectrum Framework 🤖

**Tweet 1**  
Everyone wants "autonomous AI agents."

But 80% of the value is actually in ASSISTANTS and AUTOMATIONS.

Let me explain the Agentic Spectrum—the framework we taught in Cohort 7. 🧵

**Tweet 2**  
The Agentic Spectrum has 3 levels:

**Assistants**: Human-in-the-loop, collaborative  
**Automations**: Trigger-based, rigid, reliable  
**Agents**: Autonomous, decision-making

Most people skip straight to agents. That's a mistake.

**Tweet 3**  
**Assistants** (think: ChatGPT, Claude)
- You prompt, it responds
- You're always in control
- Mistakes are cheap (just regenerate)

Example: Carlos using ChatGPT to draft outreach emails. He reviews every one before sending.

**Tweet 4**  
**Automations** (think: Zapier, Make)
- Triggered by specific events
- Follow exact rules
- No creativity, just reliability

Example: "When someone fills out this form, add them to the spreadsheet and send a confirmation email."

**Tweet 5**  
**Agents** (think: Auto-GPT, experimental frameworks)
- Autonomous decision-making
- Can take multiple actions without human approval
- High potential, HIGH RISK

Example: "Research this topic, book meetings with relevant people, negotiate terms."

Most people aren't ready for this.

**Tweet 6**  
In Cohort 7, students kept saying "I want an AI agent to do X for me."

And we'd ask: "Do you need an AGENT, or do you need an ASSISTANT with a good workflow?"

9 times out of 10, they needed the assistant.

**Tweet 7**  
The principle: Start at the lowest level of autonomy that solves your problem.

- Can a human-in-the-loop Assistant handle it? → Start there.
- Need reliability at scale? → Build an Automation.
- Truly need autonomous decisions? → THEN explore Agents (carefully).

**Tweet 8**  
J'net (Indigenomics) nailed it: "We're not ready for agents. We need assistants we can trust first."

That's wisdom.

**Tweet 9/9**  
The Agentic Spectrum helps you:
✅ Choose the right tool for the job  
✅ Manage risk appropriately  
✅ Focus on 80% value (Assistants/Automations) instead of 20% hype (Agents)

Start small. Scale smart.

#AgenticAI #AIFrameworks #Cohort7
