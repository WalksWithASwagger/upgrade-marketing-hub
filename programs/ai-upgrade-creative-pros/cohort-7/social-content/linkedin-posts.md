**Old Way:**

## Post 5: Team-Based AI Adoption (Leadership Insight)

**Headline**: Stop Sending One Person to Learn AI. Send a Team.

Here's what we learned from Cohort 7:

**Individual AI Adoption:**
- One person learns  
- Brings ideas back to the team  
- Struggles to get buy-in  
- Tool sits unused

**Team-Based AI Adoption:**
- Multiple people from the same org learn together  
- Build complementary systems  
- Create shared vocabulary  
- Hold each other accountable

**Case Study: The Indigenomics Institute**

Three people joined Cohort 7:
- **J'net**: Internal research assistant  
- **Carlos**: External outreach lab  
- **Lawrence**: Supporting infrastructure

**The result?**
A COMPLETE AI ecosystem for their organization, not isolated tools.

**Why this works:**
1. **Complementary Skills**: Different people build different pieces  
2. **Shared Language**: Everyone understands the same frameworks  
3. **Mutual Accountability**: Teams keep AI adoption on track  
4. **Sustainability**: Knowledge doesn't leave when one person quits

**For L&D Leaders:**
- Don't send your "tech person" to an AI course  
- Send a cross-functional team (ops + creative + strategy)  
- Give them time to build together, not just learn together

**The ROI:**
Individual learning = isolated tools  
Team learning = organizational transformation
