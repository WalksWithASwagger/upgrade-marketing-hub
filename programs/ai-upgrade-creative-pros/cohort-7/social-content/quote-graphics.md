## Design Guidelines

### Typography
- **Font**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, or Outfit recommended)
- **Hierarchy**: Quote text larger, attribution smaller but clear
- **Readability**: High contrast, generous line spacing

### Color Palettes

**Tech Forward** (Quotes 1, 8, 12, 14):
- Primary: Deep blue (#1E3A8A)
- Accent: Electric blue (#3B82F6)
- Background: White or light gradient

**Human Warmth** (Quotes 3, 11, 13, 15):
- Primary: Warm gray (#57534E)
- Accent: Terracotta (#E07A5F) or Gold (#F4A261)
- Background: Cream (#FAF8F3)

**Data Sovereignty** (Quotes 4, 5, 6):
- Primary: Deep earth tones (#3C2F2F)
- Accent: Traditional Indigenous colors (research appropriate)
- Background: Natural tones

**Design Professional** (Quotes 2, 7):
- Primary: Black (#000000)
- Accent: Design system pink/purple
- Background: White with subtle gradient

### Layout Options

**Option A: Full Bleed**
- Quote fills entire graphic
- Attribution at bottom
- Suggested size: 1080x1080 (Instagram), 1200x675 (Twitter)

**Option B: Card Style**
- Quote in centered card
- Subtle background pattern
- Attribution below card

**Option C: Split Screen**
- Quote on left
- Headshot or relevant image on right
- Works for instructor quotes

### Usage Recommendations

**Instagram**: Quotes 1, 3, 12, 13 (inspirational)  
**LinkedIn**: Quotes 2, 7, 10, 11 (professional)  
**Twitter**: Quotes 8, 14, 15 (punchy)  
**Website Hero**: Quote 12 or 13 (defining moments)  
**Presentation Slides**: Quotes 5, 6, 10 (framework-focused)
